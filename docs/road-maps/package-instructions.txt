RADIOPOINT — QUESNEL AREA ROAD MAPS

Three offline raster maps: Nazko.mbtiles, Blackwater.mbtiles,
Quesnel-Barkerville.mbtiles. These are regional road-reference maps, not
turn-by-turn navigation or verified forestry access maps.

INSTALL ON EACH PHONE
1. Download and extract Quesnel-Road-Maps.zip.
2. In RadioPoint, open MAPS and use the offline map archive import option.
3. Select an individual .mbtiles file, then select the imported map.
4. Pan to the relevant area. Detailed offline tiles cover zoom levels 8–14.
   If the screen is blank when zoomed in very closely, zoom out.
5. Turn off mobile data/Wi-Fi to check that the map displays offline.

Do not import this whole delivery ZIP as a tile archive. Extract it first.
No new APK is required. Actual import/display on your phones remains to be
confirmed; the SQLite archives and PNG tile contents were checked locally.

COVERAGE (approximate rectangular working areas, not official boundaries)
Nazko: west -124.00, south 52.65, east -122.40, north 53.20.
Blackwater: west -123.70, south 52.95, east -122.35, north 53.65.
Quesnel–Barkerville: west -122.65, south 52.85, east -121.30, north 53.30.
All three include Quesnel. The eastern map includes Wells and Barkerville.
Tiles touching the outer boundary may contain incomplete surrounding data.

MAP CONTENT
Gold: main roads. White: local roads. Brown/grey: tracks and minor ways.
Blue: mapped rivers and simple closed water polygons. Place/road labels
appear where space permits. The background is a neutral colour: it does
NOT classify vegetation, ownership, terrain or access rights.

Source is OpenStreetMap, fetched through Overpass API. See manifest.json for
source snapshot timestamps, feature counts, tile counts and SHA-256 hashes.
These maps were rendered directly from geographic data. No public OSM tile
server was scraped. GeoJSON source extracts are included for GIS use; they
are NOT road-track files that the current RadioPoint importer can load.

LIMITATIONS
No terrain/elevation, contours, satellite imagery, live closures, forestry
radio channels, posted kilometre calibration or verified bridge/road condition.
OSM completeness varies, particularly on forestry spurs. Some mapped ways may
be private, restricted, abandoned or impassable. Geometry does not establish
permission or suitability to drive. Check current office maps and road notices.
Complex water multipolygons were not included; lakes/rivers may be incomplete.

These files supply the visible offline map. They do not select a continuous
road track for encounter estimates. That separate GPX/KML route should be
selected and reviewed for your actual project, then imported on both phones.
Receiving crew chirps does not require a matching basemap or road track.
